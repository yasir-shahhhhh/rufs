Rufs is my next‑generation artificial intelligence system designed to behave like a live, interactive digital entity. It blends voice interaction, vision processing, and adaptive behavior into one unified interface.

Rufs isn’t a static chatbot. He’s built to feel present.

Key Characteristics

Live Avatar Interaction
Rufs speaks with a responsive, human‑like voice. Conversations feel natural, and his behavior intentionally includes subtle distractions and personality quirks — making him feel less robotic and more like a real digital companion.

Camera‑Aware Intelligence
Users can turn their camera on during a session, allowing Rufs to “see” the environment in real time — just like a video call.
He can:

Recognize objects you show him

Analyze scenes

React to facial expressions

Provide detailed, live feedback
And if the user prefers, the camera can be turned off at any moment.
